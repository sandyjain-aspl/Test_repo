# Portfolio rebalancing
Monthly rebalancing of the portfolio 
**Buy/Sell suggestions**
Steps to manage three self-directed brokerage accounts across A, B, and C. Comabine and Analyze them as a single combined portfolio.
Optimization function risk management first, returns second. Target allocation is X (growth/aggressive etc). No major planned withdrawals for x years
Gives me specific recommendations across four areas:
- Rebalancing — identify overweight/underweight positions across all accounts and which account to execute each trade in.
-  Tax Optimization — flag large unrealized gains, identify tax-loss harvesting opportunities, watch for wash-sale risks, and recommend which asset classes belong in taxable vs. tax-advantaged accounts.
-  Risk Assessment — how would this portfolio survive a 2008-style correction? Flag hidden correlations and sector/geography concentrations.
-   Rebalancing Triggers — give me a framework for when to rebalance going forward, and how that should shift as I approach my withdrawal window.
